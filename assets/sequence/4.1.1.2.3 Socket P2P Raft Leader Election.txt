title Socket P2P Raft Leader Election

participant "Node 1\n(Follower -> Candidate -> Leader)" as N1
participant "Node 2\n(Follower)" as N2
participant "Node 3\n(Follower)" as N3
participant "Local WAL" as WAL

// 1. Election Timeout & Candidate Transition
N1->N1: Election Timeout Occurred
N1->N1: Convert to Candidate, Increment Term to 1
N1->WAL: Persist currentTerm=1, votedFor=Node1

// 2. RequestVote Broadcast
N1->N2: RequestVote(term=1, candidateId=Node1, lastLogIndex=0, lastLogTerm=0)
N1->N3: RequestVote(term=1, candidateId=Node1, lastLogIndex=0, lastLogTerm=0)

// 3. Vote Validation & Response
N2->N2: Validate Candidate Term & Log
N2->WAL: Persist currentTerm=1, votedFor=Node1
N2-->N1: RequestVoteResponse(term=1, voteGranted=true)

N3->N3: Validate Candidate Term & Log
N3->WAL: Persist currentTerm=1, votedFor=Node1
N3-->N1: RequestVoteResponse(term=1, voteGranted=true)

// 4. Quorum Reached & Leader Transition
N1->N1: Votes Counted = 3/3 (Quorum >= 2 Satisfied)
N1->N1: Convert to Leader (Term 1)

// 5. Periodic Heartbeat Loop
loop Periodic Heartbeat Interval (500ms)
    N1->N2: AppendEntries(term=1, leaderId=Node1, entries=[], commitIndex=0)
    N1->N3: AppendEntries(term=1, leaderId=Node1, entries=[], commitIndex=0)
    N2-->N1: AppendEntriesResponse(term=1, success=true)
    N3-->N1: AppendEntriesResponse(term=1, success=true)
    N2->N2: Reset Election Timer
    N3->N3: Reset Election Timer
end
